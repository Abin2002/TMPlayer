TMPlayer plays the videos in your own Telegram chats and channels on an Android TV, a phone, or a Windows, macOS or Linux computer. It starts within seconds while the file is still downloading, and seeking re-aims the download instead of waiting for it.

Sign in by scanning a QR code with your phone, pick a chat, and only its playable videos appear. No messages, no server, no account except yours.

Features:
- Starts within seconds, even for large files.
- Talks to Telegram directly through TDLib, Telegram's own library. No analytics and no server run by the developer.
- Runs on Android TV, Android phones, Windows, Linux and, as a preview, Macs with Apple Silicon.
- Open source under the GPL-3.0.

TMPlayer is an unofficial client. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Telegram FZ-LLC.
