TMPlayer 1.26.0

Released 14 October 2026, for Android TV, Android phones, Windows and Linux.

What changed

  • A new Home with posters, overviews and episode names for films and shows, and TMPlayer rows on the Android TV home screen
  • A setup and a quick tour after sign in, in every language, with Performance and Mobile data steps; existing users are offered the tour once
  • Low storage mode streams videos larger than the free space, and Save mobile data fetches only a few minutes ahead on mobile data
  • Skip intro, recap and credits, speed remembered per series, preferred audio and subtitle language, watch later, season downloads, bookmarks, A-B repeat and skip silence
  • Backups of settings and lists, an app PIN and a kids lock with allowed chats
  • After a crash, TMPlayer asks before sending a report, once or always
  • A macOS preview for Apple Silicon Macs with macOS 26 or newer
  • New icons, the desktop's own window buttons, and many fixes for right-to-left languages, TV focus and long translations

Get it

This is the newest release. Installed copies offer it on their own; a new install starts from the download page.
