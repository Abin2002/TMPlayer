# Promo artwork credits

The demo titles in the promo build (and so in the screenshots on the site and in the README) are
invented, but their pictures are real photographs from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com), used under
the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). `scripts/promo-art.py` cuts each one into the
title's backdrop, its poster (the title lettered on it by TMPlayer, in Poppins) and, for the shows,
the episode thumbnails. The source PNGs live in `promo-art-src/`, which is not committed.
